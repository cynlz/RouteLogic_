Slide 5 · Strategy & Product Vision

The Strategy

The Problem Hook

Admin Clutter Forces Workarounds

Real-time fleet coordinators abandon RouteLogic mid-shift because live dispatch is buried under admin panels built for HQ. They fall back to spreadsheets and phone calls;

the largest enterprise account is trialling a leaner competitor that calls RouteLogic "an administrative burden".

The Value Proposition

Frontline-First Dispatch

Velocity gives coordinators one focused, real-time view, live vehicle status, exceptions, and one-tap reroute,

so the tool becomes the fastest way to run the shift instead of the slowest.

Data-Backed Hypothesis

"We believe a streamlined real-time dispatch view for fleet coordinators will increase daily active use during shifts, measured by a +15pt lift in coordinator DAU within 30 days, without reducing HQ admin feature usage."

Blueprint & Execution

Shift-handoff summary.

Rebuild of the HQ admin suite.

PRD Highlights
  • Dedicated View: Live vehicle map, exception queue, and one-tap reroute.
  • Fleet Defaulting: Automatically defaults to the coordinator’s assigned fleet upon login.
  • Additive Architecture: No changes to existing admin/finance modules in V1.
  • Preserves Depth: Preserves enterprise depth while accelerating real-time frontline tasks.

Slide 10 · Product Narrative & Key Retrospective

The Product Story

Friction Points & Aha Moment

The aha moment was realising the fix was subtraction, not features.

Coordinators did not need more power, they needed the live view unburied.

The hardest part was protecting enterprise depth while making the frontline the default.

Key Takeaways & What's Next
Biggest Takeaway:

For B2B tools, the workflow at the moment of decision beats feature breadth.

Next Steps:

Instrument the reroute flow and A/B test exception-alert timing to cut time-to-action further.
